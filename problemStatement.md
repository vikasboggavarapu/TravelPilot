### TravelPilot: Intelligent Trip Planning & Disruption Management Agent

### The Problem

Planning a trip involves coordinating transportation, accommodation, activities, budgets, schedules, and personal preferences.

Travel plans can also change unexpectedly due to delays, cancellations, weather, venue closures, or changes in availability.

Most travel tools handle individual bookings well but do not continuously reason across the entire itinerary when something changes.

### The Build

Build an AI-powered travel planning and itinerary management agent.

The solution should:

- Allow users to provide a destination, travel dates, budget, interests, and preferences.
- Build a day-by-day itinerary based on those constraints.
- Coordinate activities based on location, opening hours, travel time, and schedule.
- Optimize the itinerary to reduce unnecessary travel between activities.
- Track planned transportation, accommodation, and activities in one place.
- Estimate the daily and total trip budget.
- Allow users to modify constraints such as budget, duration, or interests.
- Rebuild the itinerary when a booking or activity changes.
- Detect scheduling conflicts between activities.
- Suggest alternatives when an activity becomes unavailable.
- Provide a natural-language interface for questions such as:
    - "What should I do tomorrow morning?"
    - "Can I fit this activity into today's schedule?"
    - "Which activities are close to my hotel?"
    - "What happens to my itinerary if this booking is cancelled?"
- Generate a final trip dashboard containing:
    - Daily itinerary
    - Transportation
    - Activities
    - Estimated costs
    - Important timings
    - Backup options

The goal is to build an agent that does more than recommend destinations. It should continuously manage the user's trip and adapt the itinerary when circumstances change.
